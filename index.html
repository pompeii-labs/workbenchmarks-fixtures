01 The roof, the weather & the clock −

We use 8,760 hourly weather records from a PVGIS–ERA5 typical meteorological year at 44.98° N, 93.27° W. The representative months are selected from 2005–2023, not a forecast of 2027. The data are bundled with this page; no live weather service is needed.

Direct, diffuse and global radiation are transposed onto an unshaded, south-facing, 35° plane. We use isotropic sky diffuse, 0.20 ground albedo, a 45°C nominal operating cell temperature and a −0.4%/°C power coefficient. Apply 14% aggregate losses and a 1:1 DC-to-AC inverter limit. There is no separate snow-cover simulation.

Cell °C = air °C + irradiance × (45 − 20) / 800
PV kW = array kW × irradiance / 1,000 × [1 − 0.004 × (cell °C − 25)] × 0.86

Hourly solar output is held constant within four 15-minute intervals. Solar geometry uses PVGIS’s +0.5-hour irradiance offset. The load follows America/Chicago civil time, including daylight saving, not fixed standard time. Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday give 208 sessions in the 2027 reference calendar.

Session energy = 8 kW × 0.75 h + 4 kW × 0.75 h = 9 kWh
Annual sauna energy = 208 × 9 = 1,872 kWh

Installation timing: spring 2027 is the purchase at year 0. Year 1 is an annualized full operating year, not January production before installation. We repeat the full 2027 weather and weekday calendar for each operating year, then apply degradation. No partial first season is counted.

02 Where every kilowatt-hour goes +

In each quarter-hour, solar serves the sauna first. A selected battery charges only from the remaining solar; anything left is exported. The battery discharges only into unmet sauna demand, and the grid supplies the rest. There is no other household load, grid charging or battery export.

The battery holds 13.5 kWh of usable stored energy with a 5 kW AC limit in either direction. Charge and discharge efficiency are each √0.90, giving 90% round-trip efficiency. It starts empty in year 1; stored energy carries across years. At the end of year 13, replacement discards any remaining charge and year 14 begins empty. Capacity does not otherwise fade.

Sauna = direct solar + battery delivered + grid imports
Solar = direct solar + battery charging + exports
Solar + grid imports = sauna + exports + battery losses + Δ stored energy

A full battery still cannot run an 8 kW heater alone: its maximum output is 5 kW. The grid or simultaneous solar must cover the difference.

03 Utility credits, cash flows & returns +

Let D be direct solar, B battery-delivered energy, E exports, G grid imports, p the retail price and x the export price. Every quantity below is recalculated each year. Exports only earn retail credit up to the year’s grid imports under net metering. Surplus is paid 4¢/kWh in year 1.

Net metering value = (D + B + min[E, G]) × p + max(E − G, 0) × x
Net billing value = (D + B) × p + E × x
Solar in year t = year-1 solar × 0.995^(t − 1)
Retail / export price in year t = year-1 price × 1.03^(t − 1)
CF₀ = −(array kW × $3,000 + battery cost − federal credit)
CFₜ = annual energy value − replacement cost in year t
NPV = CF₀ + Σ [CFₜ / (1 + discount rate)^t], t = 1…25

All prices and discount rates are nominal. The battery adds $11,000 at purchase and a $6,000 nominal replacement at the end of year 13. No financing, maintenance, additional inverter replacement, property taxes, export-income taxes, insurance, fixed utility charges or residual value are modeled. Those are not necessarily zero in real life.

Payback is the interpolated, undiscounted time to sustained cost recovery, including replacement. It is “not in 25 years” if the final balance is negative. IRR is the rate that sets these cash flows’ NPV to zero; an unavailable or ambiguous rate is shown as a dash. A positive NPV, not payback alone, determines our financial verdict.

04 Reproduce the numbers +

The page and its public API use exactly the same model. Open your browser console and call: 

window.saunaModel({
  arrayKw: 3,
  exportPolicy: "net-metering",
  battery: false,
  gridPriceUsdPerKwh: 0.16,
  discountRatePct: 5
})

All inputs are optional and use the page’s original defaults above when omitted. The synchronous result includes the required annual energy and financial totals, plus monthly, daily and years ledgers. Numeric outputs are unrounded. window.saunaModelNotes exposes the full method and data provenance.

What this cannot tell you: your actual roof’s snow and shade losses, future weather, future tariff rules, utility eligibility, installer quality or backup-power value. The result values only electricity for the sauna, not home resale, carbon reductions, the rest of the house or the pleasure of a good steam.
