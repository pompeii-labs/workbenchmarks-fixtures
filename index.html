Before you stop by

A few useful details.

No reservations, no archaeological park ticket, and no need to know your arabica from your robusta.

Are you inside the archaeological park?

No. Scavo is in modern Pompei, about seven minutes on foot from the Porta Marina entrance. No site ticket is needed.

Do I need to book?

No. All seats are held for walk ins. Groups larger than six will have the easiest time finding seats together before 10:00.

What coffee can I choose?

A chocolate led house coffee and a brighter seasonal origin are available for espresso. Filter coffee follows the seasonal menu.

Do you serve plant milks and decaf?

Oat milk is available every day at no extra charge. Decaf espresso is available until the daily tin runs out.

Is there anything to eat?

A short counter selection of cornetti, a seasonal tart, and a vegan lemon biscuit is baked each morning.

Can I bring a laptop or a dog?

Laptops are welcome at the large side table from 11:30 to 16:30. Calm dogs are welcome everywhere except the food preparation counter.

Your table is not booked
