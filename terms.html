The useful small print

Good coffee. Clear terms.

These notes cover visits to Caffè Scavo and use of this website. They are deliberately short and do not limit any rights you have under Italian or European consumer law.

Menu and prices

Prices are shown in euros and include applicable taxes. Seasonal coffee and baked items can change during the day. The counter team will confirm the price before preparing your order.

Allergies and dietary needs

Our small kitchen handles milk, gluten, eggs, and nuts. Oat milk and vegan recipes are available, but we cannot promise a completely free from environment. Please tell the team about an allergy before ordering.

Visiting the room

  • Seats are available to walk ins, with no minimum spend.
  • Laptop use at the large side table is welcome from 11:30 to 16:30.
  • Calm dogs are welcome away from the food preparation counter.
  • Opening hours can change on public holidays and for essential maintenance.
