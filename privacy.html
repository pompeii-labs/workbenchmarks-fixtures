Privacy, in plain language.

This page explains what happens when you visit the Caffè Scavo website. The short version is that the site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, accounts, or contact forms.

What this site stores

Nothing is stored in your browser by this site. There are no marketing cookies, preference cookies, or analytics cookies, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss.

Hosting records

The company that serves these files may keep standard security logs. These can include an internet address, browser type, requested page, and time of access. Those records are used to deliver the site and investigate faults, not to build visitor profiles.

External directions

Google Maps opens only when you choose a directions link. From that point, Google’s own privacy terms apply. The map service is not embedded here and receives no information simply because you read this page.

Your choices

  • Read the whole site without accepting cookies.
  • Use the address and opening hours without opening an external map.
  • Ask the team at the bar if you have a question about this notice.
